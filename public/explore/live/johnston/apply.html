Returned Payments

Returned Payments. If any payment made by Tenant (check, bank transfer/ACH, or other electronic payment) is returned or refused by Tenant's bank for any reason, including insufficient funds or a closed account, Tenant agrees to pay a returned-payment fee as allowed by Florida Statute 68.065: $29.00 if the payment was $50.00 or less, $34.00 if it was more than $50.00 and up to $300.00, and $35.00 if it was more than $300.00. The fee covers the returned-payment processing cost and a service charge, and is added to Tenant's account. The returned amount is due again immediately. If Tenant's automatic bank payment (autopay) is returned, autopay is turned off until Tenant sets it up again. After two returned payments in twelve months, Landlord may require future payments by certified funds or card.

RV Removal Upon Termination

Tenant has 7 day(s) to remove the RV after termination. After that, a storage fee of $25.00/day may apply.

Upon termination of this Agreement, Tenant shall remove the RV and all personal property from the lot within the number of days stated above. If the RV and/or personal property is not removed within that period, Park may treat the RV as abandoned, may charge a daily storage fee as stated above until removal, and/or may remove, store, and dispose of the RV and any remaining personal property in accordance with Florida law, at Tenant's sole expense. Park shall not be liable for any damage to the RV or personal property in connection with such removal or storage.

Park Rules & Community Guidelines

Quiet hours10 pm to 7 am every day. Keep music, TVs and generators low so everyone can rest.
Speed limit5 mph inside the park. Watch for children, pets and golf carts.
PetsUp to 3 pets. Dogs on a leash at all times outside your site. Pick up after your pet. Proof of rabies vaccine on file.
Guests & visitorsRegister guests at the office. Visitors staying more than 14 nights must be added to your household.
Your siteKeep the site clean and free of trash and stored items. No changes to pedestals, sewer or water connections.
ParkingPark only on your site. No parking on the grass or in other sites.
Trash & recyclingBagged trash goes in the dumpster by Johnston Way.
Ponds & recreationFishing in the stocked ponds is catch & release. Volleyball, shuffleboard and the Fellowship Hall are for residents and registered guests.
Fires & grillsGrills and fire pits are allowed on your site only, never left unattended. No open fires during burn bans.
Rent & paymentsRent is due on the 1st with a 5-day grace period. Pay online in your resident portal by card or bank, or set up autopay.
